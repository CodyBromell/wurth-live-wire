Livewire

Every tile is a piece of cable run. Turn them until the supply reaches every load, with no bare ends left anywhere.

Starts on Apprentice, a 5 × 5 board. Pick a harder level below.

How to play

Power every load

Lamps, sockets and motors light up when the supply reaches them. You win when all of them are live and no cable end is left loose.

Tap to rotate

Each tap turns a tile a quarter turn clockwise. Moves over the minimum cost points.

Hold to lock

Press and hold (or right-click) a tile you're sure of. Locked tiles won't turn. Tiles with four screws are already fitted correctly.

Test for faults

Shows every loose conductor for three seconds, at a 20 second time penalty.
